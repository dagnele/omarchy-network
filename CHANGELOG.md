# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Independent Network-derived widget with its own plugin and IPC namespace.
- Password eye toggle, disabled while the field is empty.
- Camera-only QR joining using Omarchy's themed Quickshell UI.
- Green scanning square and checkmark for recognized Wi-Fi codes.
- Explicit Join confirmation and feedback for unusable QR codes.
- Scan QR placed in the Wi-Fi list header and hidden while Wi-Fi is connected.
